Your work. Yours to keep.

Start on your own device. Connect and share when you choose.

Local is the starting point.

Notes and typed tables live on your own disk. Read, write, and search without an internet connection.

Different devices. The same work.

Pair your devices and sync both ways. Edits to different paragraphs can merge, so you can pick up where you left off.

Share on your terms.

Share a document, table, or agent-built site. Optional hosting in your own Cloudflare account keeps shared spaces available while your devices sleep.

Bring the AI you work with.

Connect an agent through the CLI. It reads your notes and tables, writes its work back, and re-reads your changes when you ask it to continue.

Review agent writes and undo a logical change group.
